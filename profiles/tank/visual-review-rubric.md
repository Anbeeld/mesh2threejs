# Tank visual-review rubric

Inspect matched front, side, plan, three-quarter, turntable, articulation, and diagnostic passes. Fail incorrect hull rake, turret footprint/seating, gun origin/axis, wheel rhythm, track routing, missing major fittings, floating owned parts, implausible intersections, incoherent faceting, or a macro error excused as low-poly. Name the region/view and earliest phase to reopen. Do not edit.

# Tank profile

Canonical frame: +X right, +Y up, +Z forward, ground = min Y, neutral gun +Z. A declared `sourceFrame` is executable: onboarding validates it and every prepared-oracle load applies the derived source-to-canonical rotation before user normalization; frames that require reflection are rejected. Logical ownership is a validated graph overlay independent of GLB hierarchy and is consumed by measurement, so a flat source hierarchy measures as logically nested.

Registration mechanically proves the physical frame whenever the relevant semantics exist — and only for the tank profile: wheel-chain spread along canonical Z with left/right separation on X (by structured role, not naming alone), two track courses spanning Z separated along X, neutral gun-forward alignment derived from barrel geometry against a real pivot anchor (the semantic `gun-pivot` object or an explicitly named pivot node — never the origin of whichever mesh declares it belongs to the pivot), and ground contact. A required proof that cannot be evaluated fails registration as manual-map-required. Locking tank registration requires an `oracle-sanity` capture board bound to the current preparation with re-hashed capture bytes; stale boards cannot satisfy it — builder/onboarding sanity evidence, not external visual certification.

Orientation is never granted for free: a partial candidate without turret/gun semantics must reproduce the oracle's hull-local fore/aft section-area signature, and a hull too symmetric to distinguish its own ends fails closed until directional fittings or turret/gun semantics exist.

Use only for explicit tanks or unmistakable tracked armored fighting vehicles. Required semantic model: hull/lower and upper masses, turret and pivot, gun and pivot, road wheels, two track courses, and oracle-marked identity fittings.

Tank is a sibling profile, not a thin generic extension. It composes hull-pinned curves, fourteen true-intersection contour sections (shape compared through rasterized triangle-plane intersection masks, no vertex-proximity or AABB fallback), area-weighted principal hull planes matched by normal/offset/centroid/support (tessellation independent, location aware), contact-graph contiguity, real horizontal turret contour sections (no primitive or triangle-count box detection), projected radial wheel profiles around measured axles (subdivided cuboids fail, faceted wheels pass), track course in canonical axes, and orientation proved from geometry (frame-validated). Normal gates execute only the active phase; `--global` remains the explicit complete evaluation.
